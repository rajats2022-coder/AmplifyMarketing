Privacy notice

Clear information about what the site collects and why.

This notice describes how Amplify Outreach handles information submitted through the website.

Effective August 1, 2026

The site collects only the information needed to review and respond to lead-flow audit requests.

Information collected

Details you choose to submit.

The audit form may collect your name, business name, email, phone, website or social profile, industry, service area, job values, ad spend, lead sources, goals, and free-text notes. Hosting and form-delivery providers may also process basic technical logs such as IP address, browser type, and request time.

How it is used

To review campaign fit, respond to your request, prepare the lead-flow audit, prevent abuse, and maintain the website.

What is not requested

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive personal data.

Handling and choices

Limited use, practical safeguards, and a direct request path.

Amplify Outreach does not sell personal information submitted through this site.

Service providers

Vercel hosts the site and Formspree delivers form submissions. Those providers process data only to provide their services under their own terms.

Retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable obligations.

Your requests

Use the contact form to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable recordkeeping needs.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting providers may use essential technologies and technical logs to operate and protect the service.

Updates and external links

This notice may change as the website or providers change. External sites, including social platforms, follow their own privacy practices. The effective date above will be updated when this notice materially changes.
