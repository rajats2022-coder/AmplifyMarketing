About Amplify

Built by someone who had to find customers the hard way.

Amplify Outreach began with a moving company. During his freshman year of college, founder Logan was hauling furniture, building the business, and learning firsthand how much time traditional marketing can demand.

He started sourcing leads through paid social, learned how targeted campaigns could create a more consistent pipeline, and eventually turned that experience into Amplify Outreach. Today, the same lesson guides the work: marketing should take pressure off the owner, not add another system they have to chase.
